1. Controller and Scope

This privacy policy explains how StudiesTalk processes personal data when providing a SaaS platform for schools, teachers, students, guardians, and school administrators. StudiesTalk acts as controller for its own platform operations and may act as processor where a school determines the purposes of particular student or school data workflows.

Before public or commercial launch, the controller details above must be completed with the full legal name, postal address, and privacy contact channel.

2. Categories of Personal Data

  • Account data: name, email address, role, workspace affiliation, hashed password, account status, authentication history.
  • School and class data: school name, classes, channels, membership lists, roles, timetables, permissions.
  • Attendance records: attendance status, timestamps, check-in events, late/absence markers, attendance exports and reports.
  • Learning records: homework, submissions, tests, grades, progress records, comments, certificates, classroom analytics where enabled.
  • Messages and communications: channel messages, direct messages, announcements, moderation actions, support requests.
  • Files and uploads: documents, worksheets, homework attachments, certificates, profile images, classroom resources, storage metadata.
  • Live class data: session metadata, meeting joins, waiting room actions, breakout room assignment, polls, whiteboard activity, presenter controls.
  • Recording consent and recording metadata: consent indicators, recording identifiers, playback and retention metadata, audit events.
  • AI interaction data: prompts, user instructions, generated outputs, runtime telemetry and budget controls related to AI features.
  • Technical and security data: IP address, browser and device information, login timestamps, API request metadata, security events, audit logs.

3. Purposes of Processing

  • Providing the SaaS platform and user authentication.
  • Operating classes, channels, attendance, homework, tests, reporting, and learning workflows.
  • Delivering live classes, waiting rooms, polls, whiteboard, breakout rooms, and related classroom controls.
  • Supporting AI-powered learning assistance and productivity features.
  • Managing uploaded files, classroom materials, and secure access to stored content.
  • Customer support, abuse prevention, incident investigation, fraud detection, and service reliability.
  • Maintaining security, auditing access, enforcing permissions, and monitoring misuse.

4. Legal Bases under GDPR

Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR Performance of a contract or pre-contractual measures, including account operation, class participation, homework processing, attendance, communications, and platform delivery.
Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR Legitimate interests, including platform security, fraud prevention, access control, incident response, system reliability, service improvement, and limited operational analytics.
Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR Consent where required, for example optional recording, optional cookies, or optional communications features that are not strictly necessary.
Art. 9 GDPR If special category data such as health or medical certificate information is enabled, processing must rely on explicit consent or another valid school-specific legal basis. This workflow must be separately reviewed before activation.

6. International Transfers

Where providers process data outside the EU or EEA, StudiesTalk will rely on lawful transfer mechanisms such as adequacy decisions, Standard Contractual Clauses, and supplementary safeguards where appropriate. The actual transfer setup depends on the configured providers and must be documented before launch.

7. Retention

  • Account data: generally stored until account deletion, unless longer retention is required for legal claims, audits, or school contract obligations.
  • Attendance and learning records: retained according to the relevant school retention policy and any applicable legal obligations.
  • Security logs: retained for a limited and proportionate period for security analysis, fraud prevention, and incident response.
  • Recordings: retained only according to the configured retention or deletion policy and only where recording is enabled with an appropriate legal basis.
  • Backups: retained for limited, rotating backup periods and deleted or overwritten on a defined schedule.

8. Data Subject Rights

Subject to the applicable legal conditions, data subjects have the right to request access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, portability, and to object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing is based on consent, consent may be withdrawn at any time with effect for the future.

Data subjects also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority. The specific authority should be identified based on the controller establishment and the relevant processing context.

9. Children and School Responsibility

StudiesTalk is designed for school environments. Each school or educational institution is responsible for ensuring that it has an appropriate legal basis for the student and guardian data it uses in the platform, including any guardian consent requirements that may apply under local law.

10. Recordings

Live class recordings should be off by default. If recording is enabled, participants must receive visible notice and any required consent or other lawful basis must be in place. Recording metadata and deletion rules should be configured in line with the school policy and retention schedule.

11. AI Features

AI features may process prompts, instructions, and related interaction data for service delivery depending on the configured provider. Users should not submit sensitive personal data unless strictly necessary and lawfully permitted. The exact provider terms, retention, and regional processing setup must be reviewed before production deployment.

12. Security Measures

  • HTTPS/TLS in transit.
  • Password hashing and authentication safeguards.
  • Role-based access control and tenant or workspace separation.
  • Secure file access and controlled storage permissions.
  • Audit logs and security event monitoring.
  • Administrative access restrictions and operational logging.
