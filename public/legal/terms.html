StudiesTalk supports role-based access, including school administrators, teachers, students, and other invited users. Users must keep their credentials confidential and must not share accounts. Schools are responsible for assigning appropriate roles and removing access that is no longer required.

3. Acceptable Use

Each school or educational institution is responsible for ensuring it has a lawful basis for the student, teacher, guardian, and staff data it uses in StudiesTalk. Recording features should only be used where a lawful basis exists and any required consent or notice has been obtained. Schools are responsible for configuring retention, deletion, and access permissions for recordings and related metadata.
